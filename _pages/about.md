I am **Kang Chen** (陈康), a Ph.D. student in **Mathematics** at the **Shanghai Center for Mathematical Sciences, Fudan University**, specializing in **Probability and Mathematical Statistics**. I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Fudan University in June 2026.

My research interests lie in **optimization and stochastic gradient methods**, with a focus on convergence guarantees that hold uniformly over time. My recent work studies sharp upper and lower bounds for stochastic gradient descent and the design of predetermined stepsize schedules for gradient descent.
