Yu Liu, Kang Chen, Rujun Jiang, and Tianyu Wang

With Yu Liu, Rujun Jiang, and Tianyu Wang, I investigate gradient descent with predetermined stepsizes built by recursive composition.

## Main Results

- Prove optimality of balanced splitting at every horizon within the primitive and OBS-S recursive classes, resolving a conjecture of Zhang and Jiang.
- Characterize optimal composition trees and derive an algorithm that evaluates the optimized Bellman value in logarithmic time and constant memory.
- Establish exact log-periodic convergence laws for symmetric constructions and an asymptotic counterpart for OBS-F, sharing the Silver exponent.
