Ruijie Li, Kang Chen, and Tianyu Wang

With Ruijie Li and Tianyu Wang, I study the exact time-uniform rate frontier for stochastic gradient descent on smooth convex objectives.

## Main Results

- Characterize achievable high-probability, time-uniform rates for raw SGD iterates through a necessary and sufficient summability criterion.
- Construct a horizon-free dyadic stepsize schedule and an additive conditional-restart inequality that attain every rate satisfying the criterion.
