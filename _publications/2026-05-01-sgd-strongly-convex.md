Kang Chen, Yasong Feng, and Tianyu Wang

With Yasong Feng and Tianyu Wang, I study high-probability convergence guarantees for stochastic gradient descent on strongly convex objectives.

## Main Results

- Improve the time-uniform upper bound to an iterated-logarithmic dependence on the iteration count.
- Establish a matching lower bound, showing tightness up to constants.
- Improve the last-iterate convergence rate by removing a logarithmic factor.
